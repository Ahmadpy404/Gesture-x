# Privacy Policy for Gesture X

**Effective Date**: July 2026

Gesture X is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage.

## Data Collection and Processing

- **Camera and Microphone Access**: Gesture X requires access to your device's camera and microphone to recognize hand gestures and voice commands.
- **Local Processing Only**: ALL processing of video and audio streams occurs **locally on your device**. No images, video, audio, or transcriptions are ever sent to our servers or any third-party servers.
- **No Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, or usage analytics.

## Data Retention

Since all processing is done locally and ephemerally in memory, no sensitive sensor data is retained after the extension is closed or disabled.

## Third-Party Services

Gesture X uses Google's MediaPipe (packaged locally via WebAssembly) for gesture recognition and the browser's built-in Web Speech API for voice recognition. 

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.

## Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the official repository.
